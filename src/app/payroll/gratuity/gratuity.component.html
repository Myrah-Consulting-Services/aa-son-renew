UAE Labour Law Formula
  • First 5 years: (Basic Salary ÷ 30) × 21 days × Service Years
  • Beyond 5 years: (Basic Salary ÷ 30) × 30 days × Additional Years
  • Final year: Prorated calculation for partial years
  • Unpaid leave: Deducted from total service period
  • No maximum cap: UAE Law allows unlimited EOSB amount
