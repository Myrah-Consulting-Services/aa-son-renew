Zero-rated & Exempt Supplies Information

Zero-rated supplies: VAT is charged at 0% but can be claimed as input tax. Examples include exports, international transport, certain medical supplies, and educational services.
Exempt supplies: No VAT is charged and input tax cannot be claimed. Examples include financial services, residential property, and certain government services.
